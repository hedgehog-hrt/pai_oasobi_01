The first selected trial passed all fixed confirmation criteria, including the
supplemental functional recovery criterion. Only one of the three v0.20.15
confirmation trials passed every criterion. This video does not establish
stable operation across repetitions.

In the second trial, P4 rejected 524/524 original S1 proposals during measurement.
P2 and P3 continued in independent environments. P4's two retreat actions occurred
before the displayed measurement window. Its held scene is a saved observation,
not newly captured evidence of motion or an encoding error.

Source and playback method
- These are independent LIBERO/MuJoCo simulations driven by real model inference.
  No physical robots, shared-workspace cooperation or new GPU experiments are shown.
- The source is the recorded 256 x 256 overview camera input, scaled to 360 x 360.
- Images are synchronized by the earliest recorded request that used each image.
  Those timestamps are not camera capture timestamps.
- The latest past observation is held until another observation becomes available.
  Pre-window images initialize each panel. There is no motion interpolation,
  model rerun, simulator rerender or fabricated intermediate robot motion.
- 20 fps describes the video encoding, not the original observation capture rate.
- The episode, control-step count and observation number belong to the displayed
  observation. The held duration is elapsed source time since its first use.
- Running S1 counters use the saved evaluator's measured-action selection and only
  count decisions completed by the displayed timestamp. Warmup and independent
  retreat actions are excluded. Full-window stop ratios are the saved report values.
- Status labels are derived from recorded action, retreat and episode events;
  the displayed observation may precede an action occurring at that source time.
- The two trials have different versions and trajectories. They are illustrative
  examples, not a controlled causal before/after comparison.

Sidecar files

- .frames.jsonl: encoded-frame to saved-observation mapping
